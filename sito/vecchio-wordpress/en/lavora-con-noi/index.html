Work with us

Figures we seek

Casteltech is looking for young electronic graduates / graduates who are between 18 and 35 years old.

The candidate will deal with various aspects of production with responsibilities and tasks that will be defined by the company tutor based on the knowledge and aptitudes of the same.

It is not necessary to have previously gained experience in the sector, but only interest and passion for the world

 

If you do not fit in the professional figures listed above but you believe that your c.v. can still be interesting send it to the following email address: info@casteltech.net

 

THINK DIFFERENT

 

Casteltech wants to be an operational base for New Innovators.
We make an appeal, because we believe that people with their passion can truly change the world:

"Aimed at all those young talents who have a new innovative idea in the IT / electronic field and DREAM to develop it and bring it to life, but for some reason have not yet done so .. we want to help you by making our resources available and skills.
Then why not try?
Always remember, you have to believe in yourself and in the power of your dreams."

If you are interested, send us a small PDF or Power Point presentation with your project to the email address: info@casteltech.net

You will be contacted as soon as possible.
